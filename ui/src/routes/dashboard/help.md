# How Dyad works

Dyad is a You Ship, We Ship program where you and a friend ship a software project together. Hit your hour targets, ship it, and we'll send you your pick of a two player game for the two of you to play!

## Getting started

1. Link Hackatime so your time counts.
2. Press **Add Project** and enter your friend's friend code, or send them your invite link and they can add the project from their side.
3. Pick the Hackatime projects that count toward this one, and the games you'd like, paid for with your credits.
4. Log time on the project in Hackatime (or Lapse, synced with Hackatime).

## Hour targets

You each have to spend five (5) hours on the project. If it's your first time coding and/or doing a YSWS, you only have to do three (3) hours. Your partner still has to hit their own target.

## Credits

Hitting your hour targets gets the project 2 credits to spend on games. Every extra 5 hours you each log (3 if it's your first time) gets the project 2 more, so 10 hours each is 4 credits, 15 hours each is 6, and so on. Whoever has logged less sets the pace, so keep each other going!

If you pick games worth more than 2 credits, your hour target goes up to match. Your project card shows how many credits you have and when you'll get more.

## Shipping

- Fill in the name, description, code URL, playable URL, screenshot and games before shipping.
- Press **Ready to ship** once you've hit your hour target. Your partner needs to press it too.
- Once you're both ready, the project is shipped and goes into review. You'll get a Slack DM once it's approved.
